# Fabric|Washing|Drying|Ironing|Storage|Extra care
Cotton|Machine wash cold or warm with similar colours; use a gentle detergent.|Tumble dry low or line dry; remove promptly to reduce wrinkles.|Medium to high heat when the care label allows; use steam for creases.|Fold or hang in a dry, ventilated wardrobe.|Wash dark cotton separately at first; hot water and high heat can cause shrinkage.
Denim|Turn inside out; wash cold only when needed with similar colours.|Air dry or tumble dry low; avoid prolonged high heat.|Low to medium heat inside out if needed.|Fold or hang from the waistband in a dry place.|Wash infrequently to preserve colour; spot-clean small marks.
Wool|Hand wash cool with wool detergent or use a verified wool cycle.|Do not wring; press water out in a towel and dry flat.|Use low heat with a pressing cloth, or steam from a distance.|Fold rather than hang to prevent stretching; keep clean and dry.|Avoid sudden temperature changes and agitation; check for moths.
Merino wool|Use a gentle cool wool cycle or hand wash with wool detergent.|Reshape and dry flat away from direct sunlight.|Low heat with a cloth, only if needed.|Fold on a shelf; do not hang heavy knitwear.|Air between wears; follow the garment label.
Silk|Hand wash cool with silk-safe detergent only if the label permits.|Do not wring; roll in a towel and air dry in shade.|Lowest heat on the reverse side with a pressing cloth.|Store clean, dry, and away from direct sunlight.|Avoid bleach, strong detergents, perfume, and direct heat.
Polyester|Machine wash cool or warm on a gentle cycle.|Line dry or tumble dry low; it usually dries quickly.|Low heat only; high heat can damage fibres.|Hang or fold in a clean, dry space.|Avoid high heat and fabric softener build-up.
Linen|Wash cool or warm on a gentle cycle with similar colours.|Line dry; smooth seams while damp.|Medium to high heat with steam while slightly damp if label allows.|Hang or fold with room to breathe.|Natural creasing is normal; avoid over-drying.
Rayon / Viscose|Use cold water and a delicate cycle only if the label allows; hand wash is safer.|Never wring; reshape and dry flat or hang carefully as directed.|Low heat on reverse side with a pressing cloth.|Store fully dry and avoid stretching while damp.|Fibre can weaken when wet; always check the care label.
Chiffon|Hand wash gently in cool water if washable; do not rub.|Air dry on a padded hanger away from sunlight.|Very low heat with a pressing cloth, or steam gently.|Hang in a garment bag with space around it.|Avoid snagging, rough surfaces, and heavy accessories.
Velvet|Follow the label; many velvet garments need professional cleaning.|Air dry naturally; never crush the pile while wet.|Avoid direct ironing; use gentle steam from a distance if permitted.|Hang with space; protect the pile from pressure.|Brush gently in the direction of the pile after wear.
Leather|Wipe with a slightly damp soft cloth; use a leather-specific cleaner.|Air dry naturally away from heaters and sunlight.|Do not iron.|Store on a shaped hanger in a breathable cover.|Condition occasionally with a product suitable for the leather type.
Suede|Brush dry dirt with a suede brush; use a suede-specific cleaner for marks.|Air dry away from heat; stuff lightly to retain shape.|Do not iron or steam directly.|Store dry in a breathable bag; avoid plastic sealing.|Use a suitable protector and avoid water where possible.
Nylon|Wash cool on a gentle cycle with mild detergent.|Line dry or tumble dry low.|Low heat only; high heat can melt or distort fibres.|Fold or hang in a dry place.|Keep away from hot surfaces and harsh chemicals.
Acrylic|Wash cool on a gentle cycle or hand wash.|Reshape and dry flat to reduce stretching.|Low heat with a pressing cloth if necessary.|Fold rather than hang heavy knits.|Avoid high heat and aggressive wringing.
Spandex / Elastane|Wash cool with mild detergent; avoid bleach.|Air dry or tumble dry low.|Avoid ironing unless the label explicitly permits it.|Fold loosely; avoid prolonged heat exposure.|High heat and chlorine bleach can weaken elasticity.
Satin|Use a delicate cool wash if the label permits; turn inside out.|Air dry in shade; do not twist or wring.|Low heat on the reverse with a pressing cloth.|Hang or fold carefully to avoid snags.|Keep away from rough jewellery and sharp fasteners.

# Topic|Tip
Laundry sorting|Separate whites, dark colours, bright colours, and delicate fabrics before washing.
Care labels|Check the care label before washing, drying, ironing, or using stain removers.
Zippers and buttons|Close zippers and fasten hooks before washing to reduce snagging.
Inside out|Turn printed shirts, dark denim, and embellished garments inside out before washing.
Overloading|Do not overfill the washing machine; clothes need room to move and rinse properly.
Drying|Avoid leaving damp clothes bundled together; dry them promptly to reduce odour.
Sunlight|Dry colours in shade when possible to reduce fading; use sunlight thoughtfully for suitable whites.
Ironing|Start with a lower temperature and increase only when the fabric label allows.
Storage|Store only fully dry clothes in a clean, ventilated wardrobe.
Knitwear|Fold heavy sweaters instead of hanging them so the shoulders do not stretch.
Shoes|Let footwear air out after use and rotate pairs when possible.
Repairs|Fix loose buttons, small tears, and open seams early before the damage grows.

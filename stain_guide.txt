# Stain|First action|Treatment|Avoid
Tea or coffee|Blot gently with a clean cloth; do not rub.|Rinse from the back with cool water; use a small amount of liquid detergent if the care label allows.|Do not use hot water before the stain is lifted.
Ink|Blot carefully and place an absorbent cloth beneath the stain.|Use a fabric-safe stain remover after testing a hidden area; follow its label.|Do not spread the ink by rubbing.
Oil or grease|Lift excess with a spoon or dull edge; blot.|Apply a little dishwashing liquid if safe for the fabric, then wash according to the care label.|Do not put the garment in a dryer until the stain is gone.
Blood|Rinse immediately from the back with cold water.|Use mild detergent or an enzyme stain remover if suitable for the fabric.|Never start with hot water.
Mud|Let wet mud dry, then brush off loose soil.|Rinse remaining dirt with cool water and wash as directed.|Do not grind wet mud into the fibres.
Sweat or deodorant|Rinse the affected area with cool water.|Use a colour-safe detergent; repeat gently if needed.|Avoid chlorine bleach on coloured garments.
Makeup|Lift excess product without spreading it.|Use a fabric-safe pre-treatment and wash according to the label.|Do not scrub delicate fabrics aggressively.
Chocolate|Lift solid residue with a dull edge.|Rinse from the back with cool water and apply liquid detergent if suitable.|Avoid hot water at the start.
Grass|Blot and remove loose material.|Use an enzyme-based stain remover if the garment label permits.|Test first; do not use harsh chemicals on delicate fabric.
Tomato or sauce|Remove excess sauce with a spoon and rinse from the back.|Pre-treat with liquid detergent, then wash as directed.|Do not rub the stain deeper into the fabric.
